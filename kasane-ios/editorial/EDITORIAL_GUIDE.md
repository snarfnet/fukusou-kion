# KASANE 1000 — editorial system

## Goal

Publish 1,000 place stories that change how a visitor sees the ground beneath them. The first 300 form the featured editorial core; all 1,000 must pass the same source and location gate. A famous attraction alone is not enough. Each point needs a location-specific story that can be understood while standing there.

## Selection score

Score every candidate from 0–2 in each category. A candidate needs at least 8/12.

1. **On-site revelation** — the history is not obvious without explanation.
2. **Physical trace** — something visible, walkable, or mappable survives.
3. **Time depth** — at least four meaningful moments can be sourced.
4. **Source quality** — at least two independent sources, including one authoritative source.
5. **Visitor access** — safely accessible without entering private property.
6. **Portfolio value** — improves geographic or thematic balance.

## Status workflow

`candidate → researching → drafted → translated → source_checked → location_checked → publishable`

Only `publishable` records enter the iOS bundle. Status changes never imply the next review automatically.

## Publish gate

A publishable place must have:

- Japanese, kana, and English names;
- coordinates checked to the actual story point;
- 200–400 English words in the body;
- a short summary and a location-specific headline;
- four or more sourced timeline entries;
- two to five nearby traces;
- at least two sources and one government, museum, archive, academic, or municipal source;
- a stated certainty level for the place-name origin;
- named research, English, source, and location reviewers;
- a review date;
- an explicit sensitivity category.

## Writing rules

- Separate documented fact, scholarly inference, and local tradition.
- Never convert an uncertain place-name theory into fact.
- Avoid describing living neighborhoods as frozen historical exhibits.
- Disaster, war, burial, discrimination, and religious stories require sensitive wording and an authoritative source.
- Write for someone physically present. Mention visible traces and walking direction before abstract background.
- Do not use generated text in published records.

## Source priority

1. National and municipal records
2. Museums, libraries, and archives
3. Academic publications
4. Cultural institutions
5. Wikimedia as a discovery and multilingual-link layer

Every displayed claim must be traceable through `sourceIds`.
