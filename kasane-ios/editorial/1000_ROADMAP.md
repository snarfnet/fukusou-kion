# KASANE 1000 delivery roadmap

The first 300 points are the featured editorial core. The full 1,000-point collection is still curated and reviewed, not a bulk scrape. The nationwide record layer can grow independently.

## Release batches

| Batch | Cumulative | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Foundation | 30 | Prove the workflow across every portfolio bucket |
| Travel core | 100 | Cover the main international visitor routes |
| Regional depth | 200 | Add smaller cities, landscape stories, and less obvious traces |
| KASANE 300 | 300 | Complete the featured editorial core |
| Nationwide depth | 500 | Cover every prefecture with meaningful density |
| KASANE 1000 | 1,000 | Reach a dependable nationwide collection |

Each batch ships only after all records pass the same gate. A deadline does not lower the gate.

## Definition of done

The collection is complete when:

- exactly 1,000 records are `publishable`, including the 300-record featured core;
- every bucket meets its target;
- no source URL, review date, or location check is stale under the project’s review policy;
- every sensitive story has an appropriate authoritative source and review note;
- the generated `published-places.json` passes the iOS decoding test;
- all 1,000 points have been checked on a physical map, and high-risk locations have been checked on site.

## Roles

- Researcher: establishes claims and sources.
- English editor: writes natural visitor-facing English without changing meaning.
- Source reviewer: checks every displayed claim against cited material.
- Location reviewer: verifies the coordinate represents the exact story point.

One person may fill multiple roles during prototyping. No one approves their own source review for final release.
