# Grandma Scholar at the Tobacco Shop

## Subtitle
50,000 pieces of quiet wisdom

## Description
Grandma Scholar at the Tobacco Shop is a quiet reading app filled with everyday household wisdom.

It includes 50,000 short pieces about the kitchen, cleaning, laundry, saving money, shopping, kind relationships, proverbs, seasonal living, safety at home, and caring for old tools.

The words appear slowly in a typewriter style over a nostalgic illustration of a small tobacco shop. When one piece ends, the app pauses for a breath, then begins the next one.

There are only simple controls: previous, next, random, pause, and play. Open it during a short break, before sleep, or while resting between chores.

This is a paid app and does not show ads.

Japanese and English data are stored separately. If your device language is English, the app shows the English wisdom set.

This app is for general reading and everyday inspiration. It is not medical, legal, or professional advice.
